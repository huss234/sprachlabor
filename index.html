GitHub connection

              
The token is kept in this browser only and is sent nowhere but GitHub, over an encrypted connection.
Never paste it into a message, a file or the public repository. Anyone holding it can read and write your private folder. If it leaks, delete it on GitHub and make a new one.

              

                

                  
                  
                

                

                  
                  
                

              

              

                
                
              

              

                
                
                
                
              

            


            

              
Automatic sync

              
It syncs after you study, when you reopen the app, and every minute while it is open. Off, it syncs only when you ask.
Devices never overwrite each other. Lessons merge one by one and the newest edit wins; progress merges word by word, and your history pools what each device recorded.

What it does

              

                
LibraryYour files, in folders you make. A file is a passage with its cards, or a deck of cards on its own.

                
LessonThe German text, with the English hidden, line by line, or in full. Tap any word to open its card.

                
CardsEvery card you own, in decks by how well you know it: hard, learning or known.

                
SpellingYou see the English and type the German — capitals, umlauts and der, die, das included.

                
ProgressDaily targets, your streak, milestones and records, a calendar, and the time you have studied.

                
SettingsHow it looks, how it sounds, and where your files live.
