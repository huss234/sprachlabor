Automatic sync

When this is on, the app syncs quietly after you study and whenever you reopen it. Turn it off to sync only with the button above.

How the merge works

Your two devices never overwrite each other wholesale. Lessons merge one by one and the newest edit wins. Progress merges word by word, so a session on your phone and a session on your tablet both survive. Your study history is append-only, which means it cannot conflict at all — the two devices simply pool what each of them recorded.

Data management

Every lesson is one JSON file — a paragraph with its cards, or a deck of cards on its own. Make them with the prompts, bring them in, take them out.

Turn any German content into a file

There are two ways to study here, so there are two prompts. Copy one, paste it into whichever assistant you use, then paste your German underneath it. What comes back is a file this app imports as it stands — dropped in below, or pasted straight into the box.

Paragraph prompt

Builds a reading passage and its deck inside one file: the German text, the English line by line, and one card for every single word in it — articles, particles and one-letter words included. Use it for anything you want to read.
