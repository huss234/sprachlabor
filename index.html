How the merge works

        
Your two devices never overwrite each other wholesale. Lessons merge one by one and the newest edit wins. Progress merges word by word, so a session on your phone and a session on your tablet both survive. Your study history is append-only, which means it cannot conflict at all — the two devices simply pool what each of them recorded.
